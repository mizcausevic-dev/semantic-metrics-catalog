# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-14

### Released
- Published **semantic-metrics-catalog** as a governed analytics definition layer for metrics, owners, and contract posture.
- Added overview, catalog, contract board, owner lanes, JSON-LD export, README proof surfaces, tests, and CI.
- Positioned the repo around semantic reliability for both human operators and AI retrieval systems.

### Why this mattered
- Metric ambiguity usually appears as trust erosion long before it appears as a broken query.
- Executive dashboards, RevOps decks, and AI systems all benefit when definitions travel with ownership and freshness context.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-02-11

### Shipped
- Locked the first internal contract board pattern for owner accountability, freshness posture, and policy-flag review.
- Proved that metric definitions could be published as a browsable control surface instead of staying buried in modeling files.

## [Prototype] - 2025-06-06

### Built
- Built the earliest prototype around metric contracts, formula visibility, and semantic publishing for analytics stakeholders.
- Tested whether explicit contract state could reduce semantic drift during operating reviews.

## [Design Phase] - 2024-09-17

### Designed
- Chose a contract-first framing instead of another thin metrics dictionary.
- Treated ownership, freshness, and policy posture as core product surfaces rather than optional metadata.

## [Idea Origin] - 2023-04-28

### Observed
- The same business metric names were being reused across teams while meaning slightly different things.
- The missing artifact was a governed catalog that made metric meaning portable enough for both humans and AI systems.
